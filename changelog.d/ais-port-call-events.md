- Ship calls can now be rebuilt from AIS tracks: when the first tug made
  fast, when the ship was all fast at its berth, when it let go and when the
  last tug released, with the pilot transfer inferred from tug contact
  outside the breakwater. Each call gets ship, tug and pilot emissions that
  respond to the scenario (shore power at berth, electric or Tier 4 tugs).
  Tested on NOAA AIS for 11–17 June 2023 at Port of Hueneme (11 calls).
