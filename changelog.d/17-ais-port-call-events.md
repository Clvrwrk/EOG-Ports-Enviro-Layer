- Ship calls can now be rebuilt from AIS tracks: when the first tug made
  fast, when the ship was all fast at its berth, when it let go and when the
  last tug released. Each call gets ship and tug emissions that respond to
  the scenario (shore power at berths 1 and 2, the only berths that have it,
  and electric or Tier 4 tugs). Berths are matched to the wharf faces in the
  port's June 2025 terminal plan, so car carriers now show at berth 4. Port of
  Hueneme needs no pilot, so no pilot boarding or pilot boat is counted there.
  Tested on NOAA AIS for 11–17 June 2023 at Port of Hueneme (11 calls).
