- The port asset registry now covers the whole Port of Hueneme: berths,
  buildings and tanks, yards, gates, rail spurs, locomotives, tugs and harbor
  craft, each from a named public source. Every asset class lists its fields
  for the admin portal, and port staff's own records load from a private
  overlay (#11).
- Hueneme berths follow the port's May 2025 At Berth terminal plan: Berths 1
  and 2 (South Terminal) take reefer and tanker ships and have ship shore power,
  out since the December 2023 storm and due back late 2027 to early 2028;
  Berths 4 and 5 (North Terminal) take RoRo ships, with shore power planned for
  2028; the other berths sit on the Navy Terminal. Reefer calls no longer go to
  Berth 3 (#11).
