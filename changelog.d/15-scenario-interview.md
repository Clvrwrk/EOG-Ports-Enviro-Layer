- /scenarios has "Build by interview": an AI interviewer asks one question at
  a time, each chosen from the last answer, and builds a scenario that loads
  into the builder, the compare table and the 3D port. It falls back to a
  guided, no-AI interview when the AI route is unavailable. Scenarios can now
  also carry a percent rate change (energy, demand, customer charge) and
  delivered energy covering a share of port load (share, baseload, solar or
  evening-peak profile, priced per kWh).
